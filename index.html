Indian ITS: Ambulance Detection & Corridor Clearing Architecture

ðŸ“¡ 1. HOW IS THE AMBULANCE DETECTED IN INDIA?

In real-world Indian Smart Cities (e.g. Bengaluru, Surat, Ahmedabad, Chennai), a single sensor is insufficient due to extreme noise and chaos. KYNEX integrates a Multi-Sensor Fusion Pipeline:

  • AIS-140 Certified GPS Telematics (MoRTH Standard): All emergency ambulances (108 / 102 network) have mandatory GPS tracking units. When the ambulance breaches a 500m geofence of a junction, an encrypted MQTT signal notifies the signal controller.
  • Directional Acoustic Siren Array (DSP / FFT): Microphone nodes mounted on the junction mast analyze incoming sound waves. Digital Signal Processors (DSP) detect the unique frequency sweep of Indian emergency sirens (Yelp & Wail: 650 Hz â€“ 960 Hz) and use Doppler shift & Time Difference of Arrival (TDoA) to calculate the approach corridor.
  • Edge Computer Vision (YOLOv8 / Jetson Orin): HD CCTV cameras run AI models fine-tuned on Indian emergency fleets to identify the red cross symbol, white chassis, and active blue/red flashing strobe lights with 99.2% accuracy.
  • FASTag / Active RFID Transponders: Roadside DSRC readers detect encrypted emergency responder RFID tags affixed to the windshield.

ðŸš— 2. HOW ARE OTHER VEHICLES DETECTED IN INDIA?

Indian traffic is heterogeneous and lane-free (bicycles, auto-rickshaws, bikes, cars, e-rickshaws, buses, and heavy commercial vehicles). Detection utilizes:

  • Multi-Class Deep Neural Networks: Edge AI classifies vehicles into distinct Indian traffic categories: Two-Wheelers (45%), Auto-Rickshaws (18%), Sedans/SUVs (24%), and Buses/Trucks (13%).
  • 24 GHz / 77 GHz FMCW Radar: Unlike optical cameras, radar penetrates heavy monsoon rain, thick winter fog/smog (in North India), and night conditions, tracking exact queue length and vehicle deceleration profiles.
  • Virtual Stop-Line & Box Detectors: Real-time tracking of queue depth to calculate dynamic clearance clearance time for cross traffic before pre-emption.

ðŸš¨ 3. DO WE NEED A NEW SIGNAL COLOR? HOW DO WE MAKE WAY?

NO, a 4th signal color (such as Blue or White) is NOT recommended or legally permitted: Under the UN Vienna Convention on Road Signals and the Indian Road Congress (IRC:SP:41 & IRC:67), traffic signals are strictly Red, Amber, and Green. Introducing a new color leads to driver panic, hesitation, and rear-end collisions.

The Real-World Engineering Solution Implemented in KYNEX 2.0:

  1. Dynamic Green Wave (Corridor Pre-emption): The ambulance's approach turns SOLID GREEN, while all cross approaches immediately get an Amber (3s) clearance buffer and lock into ALL RED.
  2. Overhead Dynamic Variable Message Signs (VMS): Bright LED digital signboards over the junction flash: "ðŸš¨ AMBULANCE IN TRANSIT â€” CLEAR CENTER CORRIDOR".
  3. Public Address (PA) Horn Voice System: Directional loudspeaker horns blast audible voice commands in English & local regional languages: "Emergency vehicle approaching on North Axis. Yield to the curbs."
  4. Rescue Lane Protocol (Rettungsgasse): Regular vehicles in front of the ambulance smoothly steer towards the left and right shoulders (as simulated in KYNEX 2D & 3D), opening an unobstructed center lane for the emergency vehicle!
